About the Club

Built for campus sport, team culture, and open participation.

Organization of KUET Sports coordinates training, campus fixtures, and student-driven tournaments so every department can compete with structure and support.

Mission

Grow sports participation across KUET with a fair, active, and welcoming program.

What we run

Trials, fixtures, tournaments, captain selection, and match-day coordination.

Who can join

Any KUET student interested in training, competing, or helping organize events.

Club Structure

How the club stays active

A simple operating rhythm keeps sports activity moving across the semester.

1. Trials

We open trials for new players and assign team captains based on performance and commitment.

2. Training

Each team follows a practice schedule with warm-ups, drills, and match preparation.

3. Fixtures

Fixtures are published ahead of time so players can prepare, travel, and compete on time.
