About the Club

Built for campus sport, team culture, and open participation.

Organization of KUET Sports coordinates training, campus fixtures, and student-driven tournaments so every department can compete with structure and support.

Mission

Grow sports participation across KUET with a fair, active, and welcoming program.

What we run

Trials, fixtures, tournaments, captain selection, and match-day coordination.

Who can join

Any KUET student interested in training, competing, or helping organize events.
